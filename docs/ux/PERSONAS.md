# Personas

These are research hypotheses, not validated personas. **EVIDENCE GAP – REAL USER SURVEY REQUIRED.**

| Persona | Context | Goals | Risks/needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reception coordinator | busy front desk, interruptions | find booking, check in, move queue quickly | avoid wrong patient/facility; clear conflicts |
| Clinician | time-constrained consultation | see relevant context, document encounter | clinical accuracy, draft ownership, minimal taps |
| Nurse | supports multiple clinicians | monitor queue/encounters, read notes as allowed | role clarity and current status |
| Clinic manager | configures services/staff | oversee operations and access | auditability, least privilege |
| Patient (future) | personal device, varied literacy | book and understand approved information | privacy, language and accessibility |

Recruit representative participants and update goals, vocabulary, environment, device constraints and accessibility needs from evidence. Do not merge staff and patient experiences into one trust model.
