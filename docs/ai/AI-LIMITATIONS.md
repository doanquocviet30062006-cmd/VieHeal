# AI limitations

The target assistant is probabilistic and may omit, misinterpret or invent information, especially with sparse, ambiguous, long or domain-specific Vietnamese text. Structured JSON improves integration but does not prove clinical correctness. Provider availability, latency and model changes can affect results.

It is not a medical device claim, diagnostic system, prescriber, emergency service or final record authority. It sees only explicitly selected context and therefore lacks the complete clinical picture. Clinicians remain responsible for review and saved content.

Known open items: provider/model selection, approved data region/retention, cost/token budget, evaluation thresholds, clinical reviewer availability, fairness assessment, incident/rollback process and longitudinal monitoring.
