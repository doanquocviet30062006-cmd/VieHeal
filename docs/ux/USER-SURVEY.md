# User survey

**EVIDENCE GAP – REAL USER SURVEY REQUIRED.** No responses or numbers are claimed.

## Ethical recruitment

Recruit clinic staff, medical/nursing students with workflow exposure, clinicians and potential patients as appropriate. Obtain voluntary consent, avoid collecting patient records or sensitive employment data, anonymize responses and explain academic use. Obtain institutional approval if required.

## Questionnaire

1. What is your role and how often do you interact with clinic appointments/queues/records?
2. Describe the most time-consuming step in booking or check-in.
3. How do you know whose turn it is and where does that fail?
4. What information is essential on a mobile appointment/queue screen?
5. What errors occur when moving from check-in to consultation?
6. How do network loss or slow systems affect work?
7. Which tasks should never be available offline?
8. Rate comfort with viewing patient information on a staff phone (1–5); explain.
9. What privacy/security controls would increase trust?
10. Would a documentation draft assistant help? In which exact task?
11. What AI behavior would be unacceptable or unsafe?
12. Should AI-generated text require explicit clinician confirmation? Why?
13. Test the prototype: can you complete [assigned scenario]? Where did you hesitate?
14. Rate clarity, speed and confidence (1–5 each) and explain low ratings.
15. What single change would most improve the workflow?

## Data collection template

`participant_id, cohort, date, consent, device familiarity, Q1…Q15, task_success, time_on_task, observed_errors, notes`. Store consent separately and restrict access.

## Analysis template

Report recruitment method, sample size, cohort distribution, missing data and limitations. For scaled questions show count/median/range; for open text code themes with two reviewers where possible. Link each finding to a requirement/design change and counterexample. Do not generalize beyond the sample or manufacture percentages. Attach a redacted response/export as rubric evidence.
